Contex of the problem in the Manteinance request related to biomedical equipments in Hospitals.

The Dominican Republic has great potential to achieve better standards in healthcare services. To make this potential a reality, it is necessary to investigate common problems and propose simple and effective solutions that have not been implemented before.

In Santo Domingo, the public hospital network manages a large amount of medical equipment but lacks sufficient biomedical engineers to address nonconformities and technical issues. In the main office, only one engineer has the knowledge required to evaluate and solve these problems. However, the current reporting process is extremely inefficient, one report can take up to 14 days to reach the main office due to physical paperwork and bureaucratic delays.

To simplify the reporting process and reduce the time it takes to receive technical information, the proposed solution is to create a web-based reporting form. Hospitals will be able to report equipment nonconformities directly to the main office, reducing delays and eliminating the risk of losing physical documents.

The form will be designed to be user-friendly and accessible, allowing hospital staff to quickly and easily report issues with biomedical equipment. It will include fields for essential information such as the type of equipment, the nature of the problem, and any relevant details that can help the biomedical engineer diagnose and address the issue, and including go prepared with refacciontions that may solution the problem.
